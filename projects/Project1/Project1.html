Web Site Critiques

Trader Joe's - https://www.traderjoes.com/home

  1. What was your first impression of this site?

    My first impression of the site what that it was good-looking and on-theme for Trader Joe's. I also noticed how it was relatively simple, and I could see how this could be a simple creation for an html expert.

  2. How does this site organize the flow of information? (Do they have links on the left and text on the right? Some other scheme? No scheme?)

    I actually think the information does flow pretty well here. There are important links on the left side (Products, Discover, Listen, etc.) and everything flows from top to bottom in order of importance from my perspective. The scheme seems to match their brand, and currently has a fall theme for the season which makes sense to me.

  3. Does the site structure make sense or does it confuse you? Why?

    The site structure does make sense to me for the most part. As I stated before, I think most things are ordered by importance and I can see that even though I'm not a Trader Joe's shopper. One thing that does confuse me is how hard they push their podcast. They don't immediately explain on the site why someone would listen to a Trader Joe's podcast.

  4. Does the site graphics/layout/multimedia enhance the site or detract from it? Why?

    For the most part the site's graphics does enhance the sight. Especially because of the fall theme they have going on, the whole energy of the page's graphics makes me want to buy fall products like apple cider donuts. The only multimedia I did not like was the few pop-ups it gave me in the beginning. They felt redundant and annoying.

  5. How long did it take for the home page to load? Was the load time reasonable?

    The page loaded basically immediately. It was less than 2 seconds for sure. The load time is totally reasonable to me.

  6. Was the text color and size readable? If a background color or image was used, did it enhance or detract from the site?

    The text color and size was readable. I'd say it was a standard 12-18 point font and standard black. There was no background color, it was the plain white. I think that is totally fine for Trader Joes' page.

  7. Did the site have a navigation bar or other navigation tool? Was the navigation well done?

    The site had the standard built-in navigation bar to the right that basically every page has. I think it is simple and effective. It is better to not reinvent the wheel.

  8. Did the site have a color scheme or look and feel that was repeated on all the pages?

    As stated before, the whole page has a fall feeling. It was repeated on all of the pages as I navigated around the site.

  9. How does the site respond to smaller window sizes (responsive design)?

    I actually did most of this assignment with the website on the right half of my monitor, and it adapted completely fine. I found no issues and it still fits the space well.

  10. What does this site offer that was unusual or very well done?

    The site offers you to be able to click a play button to listen to their latest podcast episode. I think that is quite abnormal. I don't think this really is the best idea, but I'm sure this was an idea of higher management rather than the website designers.
